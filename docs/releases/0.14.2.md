## ALO 0.14.2

Release candidate — signed packaging and release validation are in progress; not yet published.

ALO 0.14.2 introduces the secure nearby room runtime, an iOS companion target, and shared screen annotations alongside synchronized-playback improvements. It follows the separately published 0.14.1 Mac release.

- New secure rooms use per-installation identities, pinned mutual TLS control channels, and authenticated encrypted media and voice datagrams. Existing rooms retain an explicit legacy transport policy; secure connections do not silently downgrade.
- Discover nearby rooms using supported local-network and peer-to-peer paths. An internet connection or shared Wi-Fi access point is not always required, but Wi-Fi radios and OS-supported connectivity still are. Bluetooth alone is not the media transport.
- Add the iOS companion source target for room discovery, listening, viewing shared screens, chat, directed voice, and viewer annotations. The Mac download does not install the iOS app; device installation requires its own signing and provisioning.
- Add shared annotation tools and synchronized presenter/viewer scenes with authenticated commands, bounded recovery, and source-generation isolation.
- Keep directed voice separate from room media mute. Sending microphone audio requires explicit local action; an incoming invitation never opens the recipient's microphone automatically. Music ducking is optional and disabled by default.
- In secure rooms, each device controls its own media level; per-person received voice levels remain local controls. Remote media-level controls are unavailable rather than appearing to change another device without an authenticated command.
- Coordinate hardware-latency increases at a future capture boundary. A listener that misses that boundary recovers its own output without restarting healthy listeners.

- Add a room game library with Rift Arena for solo practice, room duels, and spectating, plus Fourfold for bot or local pass-and-play. Downloaded packs are verified, data-only presentation content; the native game engines remain part of the signed app.
- Expand room chat with replies, reactions, author-only edit/delete, collaborative pins, search, mentions, notification preferences, and retained drafts. Return now submits reliably, while failed sends keep the draft and explain the missing room connection.
- Add persistent per-person voice levels, clearer device state, music ducking controls, and an opt-in local microphone test. See the channel-by-channel privacy notes for secure and legacy room differences.

- Mute room media now silences only received room audio. It no longer silences this Mac's own broadcast playback. The incoming mute preference is retained when switching between broadcasting and listening; voice and per-device mixer controls remain separate.
- Clicking the menu-bar icon now shows or hides Spaces when outside a room, preserving unfinished input. Inside a room, the existing controls popover still toggles normally.
- Give the Talk bar a pitch-black background with legible controls in either system appearance. Keep the unread-message badge fully visible above its action group, including two-digit counts.
- Restore full-width artwork-derived gradients in the media header, with readable light/dark appearances and a neutral fallback when no colourful artwork is available.
- Turn the seam below the media header into the room's playback progress line. A restrained album-colour flare advances with the track, freezes when paused, and falls back to a quiet divider when macOS does not expose timing.
- Preserve short audio-capture and send-completion bursts without discarding their waiting packets. Bound waiting audio by size and age, and discard stale data under sustained congestion. Clear waiting audio on pause, resync, disconnection, and stop.
- Account for observed local send-completion time when admitting waiting audio, leaving room for playback scheduling. Recover after a stalled path becomes idle. This is a local congestion estimate, not a remote-delivery guarantee.
- Report submitted, expired, admission-rejected, replaced, and transition-discarded audio packets in diagnostics. Prevent already-expired waiting packets from making fresh capture look congested.
- Prevent a late listener's network delay from being treated as speaker/headphone hardware latency and repeatedly restarting the entire room's audio timeline.
- Preserve a stable timing allowance across small hardware-latency adjustments and short pauses. Add per-listener timing diagnostics to distinguish network delay from output-device latency.
- Always clean up a detached broadcaster when rapid room or broadcast transitions cancel older work.
- Bound screen-video send, decode, and presentation queues; recover dropped video with a fresh keyframe instead of accumulating delayed frames.
- Keep screen presentation bounded through the UI-thread handoff too. A delayed UI presents the latest due frame instead of accumulating stale image updates.
- Report fresh audio render drift and screen UI-handoff lateness in diagnostics. Connected, stale, or missing timing measurements no longer count as verified synchronization. Include blocked-UI, drift-warning, recovery, and static-screen regressions.
- Carry relative screen-handoff timing back to the broadcaster, so healthy audio cannot hide a listener's delayed screen. Old peers without screen measurements remain unverified during screen sharing.
- Update the visible sync verdict automatically, without blocking the UI on diagnostic reads. Retain a bounded, redacted history of sync warnings and recovery in shared diagnostic reports.
- Protect secure-room control/video with authenticated TLS and audio with authenticated, replay-protected packets. Public secure rooms are encrypted but remain open to admission; encryption does not make a public room private. Legacy private media retains its separate shared-secret protection. Update participating devices before creating a secure room.
- Harden clock probes, packet decoding, and room-state counters against stale or malformed input.
- Pace large room-history transfers, bound retained live history, and coalesce disk writes. Prevent queued writes from restoring a forgotten room.
- Update saved room secrets without deleting the existing Keychain entry first. Prevent delayed artwork requests from overwriting newer playback state.
- Retain the Spaces, shared room-icon, and update-indicator changes from 0.13.45 and 0.13.46.
- Fix the album-colour playback seam when macOS reports a timestamp but leaves its elapsed-time value at zero. Progress now starts at the real track position, advances while playing, freezes while paused, and stays bounded to the track duration.
- Keep room navigation available in the compact controls and make Talk targeting explicit without adding persistent visual noise.
- Improve chat with typed mention suggestions, Command-K search, a smaller multiline composer, and clearer collapsed-message previews.
- Move microphone testing into Settings, add optional lyrics and Touch Bar room controls, and expose local audio-drift correction without changing another member's volume.

Validation includes real localhost room/network scenarios, deterministic capture/link simulations using the real host sender, controlled sender stalls and callback bursts, private TLS and encrypted audio, and synthetic H.264 encode/decode. Physical multi-Mac, Bluetooth, and RF testing is still needed; this release does not claim that every device-specific audio issue has been reproduced. The newly reported long-running YouTube screen/audio drift is not yet confirmed resolved on physical devices.

Secure rooms use installation-identity pins and TLS session credentials; private admission additionally requires the room secret. First-contact trust still matters. Legacy private-media protection uses its existing shared secret and must not be confused with the secure runtime's identity model.

Includes the published 0.13.49 audio-backlog, media-mute, and menu-bar fixes. The required CI suite retains live capture timing, full packet accounting, congestion limits, and deliberate scheduler-delay tests. A strict capture timer avoids artificial headless wake coalescing without dropping source chunks or relaxing timing limits.
