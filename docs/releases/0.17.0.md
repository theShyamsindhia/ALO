# ALO 0.17.0

A personal smoking log to understand timing and cost, with optional daily summaries for your team.

## Log quickly

- Enable **Smoking log** in **Pin to menu bar**, or open **Spaces → your profile menu → Stats · Smoking…**.
- Choose Classic Connect (₹430 per 20; ₹21.50 each) or Marlboro Clove Mix (₹240 per 10; ₹24 each).
- Log now or an earlier time, optionally add context, and edit or delete entries. Quick logging and deletion support undo.

## See the pattern

- Day view places each cigarette on a time-of-day chart. Week and Month show daily counts or estimated consumption cost over 7 or 30 days.
- See the number logged, estimated cost, and typical within-day gap. Overnight gaps are excluded from that median.
- Prices are saved with each entry, so future price changes do not rewrite past costs. Costs represent cigarettes logged, not pack purchases; an empty day means no entries, not confirmed abstinence.

## Private by default

- Each ALO profile has its own local log on this Mac. Logs do not sync between devices, and ALO Dev keeps separate records from regular ALO.
- **Share today’s summary…** previews the destination and daily count/cost before posting one message to your current channel. Exact timestamps and context tags remain private. Recipients can retain the message.
- No automatic broadcasts, leaderboards, or reminders to smoke.

Available in the Mac app. Room audio synchronization and screen-video timing are unchanged.
