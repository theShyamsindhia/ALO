# ALO 0.15.9

The approved Spaces and conversation window, with native frosted glass and more consistent geometry.

- Native behind-window frosted material, without the transparency fade that exposed sharp content underneath. The backdrop stays active when the window loses focus and respects Reduce Transparency.
- Rounded outer corners and a matching inset conversation panel, without the competing window edge.
- Inset native traffic lights with working hit targets and consistent spacing above Spaces.
- Stable typography across window sizes, aligned sidebar rows, and a separate now-playing footer carried forward from the unreleased 0.15.8 candidate.
- Regression coverage for compact layouts, window resizing, corner clipping, native controls, and backdrop configuration.

Chat behavior, room controls, audio synchronization, and video timing are unchanged.

## Verification

The UI was approved in ALO Dev and all nine targeted native-window tests passed locally. Full optimized Mac tests, room scenarios, the iOS build, signing, notarization, and clean-machine distribution checks remain required before stable publication. The earlier eight-listener audio stress failure is not waived by UI approval.
