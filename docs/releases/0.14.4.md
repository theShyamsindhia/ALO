## ALO 0.14.4

ALO 0.14.4 completes the reviewed fixes that arrived immediately after 0.14.3.

- Keep DJ conversion, live overlays, and room fan-out off the real-time audio render callback, and make its regression test deterministic under CI scheduling.
- Cancel a pending DJ broadcast when the studio closes instead of allowing a silent headless broadcast to claim the room.
- Harden room attachments with immutable sender-scoped cache identities, sender/message correlation, authenticated capability checks, bounded per-peer receive admission, transfer cleanup, and isolated development storage.
- Restore valid cached attachments after room rejoin or relaunch. Received images are no longer decoded automatically inside chat rendering; files remain available to open explicitly.
- Remove the opaque outer canvas from the default app icon and all 16 selectable variants. The generated multi-size Finder, Dock, and notification icon now has transparent corners.
- Preserve the 0.14.3 live-broadcast recording decks, rewind/loop controls, room settings, file sharing, and lyrics integration.

Validation includes release-mode DJ and attachment tests, a secure localhost transfer scenario, packaged-icon extraction, a clean independent review, and the required strict Mac/iOS CI gates. Signed and notarized artifacts are produced only by the release workflow.
