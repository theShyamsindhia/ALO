# ALO 0.17.3

Seamless peer-to-peer media, clock synchronization, and room canvas recovery over Apple Wireless Direct Link (`awdl0`).

## Room sync & peer-to-peer transport

- **AWDL media & canvas channels (`awdl0`):** Secondary peer channels (`.mediaControl`, `.video`, `.voiceControl`, `.roomCanvas`, and `.fileTransfer`) as well as mesh peer directory hints now dial the peer's Bonjour service endpoint (`_alo-peer._tcp.local.`) whenever the peer's link-local path traverses `awdl0`. This prevents macOS Skywalk/NECP from dropping incoming `SYN-ACK` packets to the BSD kernel TCP stack on networks with Wi-Fi AP/client isolation, allowing live audio/video synchronization and collaborative canvas sessions to connect immediately.
- **Fast stale-link eviction:** Direct peer links that stop receiving heartbeats (for example, when a peer closes their MacBook lid or goes out of range) are now retired after the liveness lease expires instead of waiting for TCP retransmission timeouts, unblocking immediate room presence updates and clean reconnection.
- **Unbounded reconnect recovery:** When initial reconnect backoff attempts are exhausted while a room has no active peers, ALO refreshes the Bonjour scan window and periodically re-scans until peers return.
