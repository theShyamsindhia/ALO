# ALO 0.15.7

A new native Spaces and conversation interface, based on the approved design.

- A clearer sidebar for networks, channels, nearby spaces and your profile, with search and a now-playing card connected to the playing channel.
- A rounded conversation surface with system materials, light and dark appearances, and a single header for channel actions.
- Clearer message grouping, blue outgoing messages, highlighted mentions and inline image previews.
- An anchored composer and responsive layout, with regression coverage for scrolling, unread messages and compact windows.

Existing room actions, replies, editing, reactions, pins, attachments and notification controls remain connected. Compact/notch chat retains its existing presentation. This release does not change audio synchronization or video timing, and does not claim to fix the receiver video lag currently under investigation.

Full Mac tests, room scenarios, the iOS build, signing, notarization and clean-machine distribution checks must pass before this candidate becomes the stable update.
