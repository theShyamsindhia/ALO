## ALO 0.13.45

- Spaces no longer highlights the previously remembered room or preselects a
  newly discovered space. Keyboard selection remains available for navigation.
- Adds matching circular Sync and New Space controls. Sync reloads saved rooms
  and restarts nearby discovery without joining a room or starting a broadcast.
- Adds the installed version and Check for Updates to the Spaces footer, using
  the existing signed-update flow.
- Adds twelve native room-icon choices under Space options → Shared Icon.
  Choices are saved locally and shared with other updated Macs when you join.
  Authenticated peers converge on one versioned icon, including late joiners;
  simultaneous edits resolve deterministically without changing room history.
- Nearby public spaces show participant names and the current track when
  advertised by an updated Mac. Hover for full details. Private spaces do not
  advertise member names, track titles or icons before authentication.
- Deduplicates nearby participants across network interfaces. Older clients
  retain the existing participant-count fallback and ignore icon updates.

Verified native light/dark layouts, private invite entry, room-icon persistence
and three-peer sharing, discovery compatibility/privacy, updater validation,
chat scrolling and the room network/lifecycle scenarios. No audio, playback or
voice-processing behavior changes.
