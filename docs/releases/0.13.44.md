## ALO 0.13.44

- Makes Spaces a native SwiftUI list with standard typography, separators,
  selection, text fields, buttons and a private-space toggle.
- Removes the fixed green palette, painted outer border and custom picker
  button styles. System material, light/dark appearance, accent color and window
  shadow now provide the chrome.
- Keeps the compact artwork/profile layout and existing create, join, rename,
  invite-key and forget actions. The profile-name button uses native styling too.
- Keeps a selected private room visible above its invite-key field and supports
  Return to open the selected room or focus its invite-key field.

Includes native rendering coverage for populated, empty, private and creation
states in light and dark mode. Chat, playback and voice transport are unchanged.
