# ALO 0.14.6

Breach now supports room multiplayer and the complete demolition match loop: four seats across two teams, bot-filled lobbies, ready/start, joining and spectating, late joins at the next round, weapon and armor purchases, round economy, bomb planting/defusing, team scoring and rematches. Tab shows player statistics; controls and graphics/audio settings are configurable.

The match host validates gameplay. Guest movement prediction, remote interpolation, bounded snapshots and priority action packets keep room traffic controlled. Host departure leaves the ALO room intact. Connect 4 is hidden from the library, and Stick Fight remains available.

Includes compiler compatibility fixes for the Stick Fight release build.

Open Breach from an ALO room to play together. All participants need this version for Breach multiplayer. Standalone practice remains available offline.
