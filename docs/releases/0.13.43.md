## ALO 0.13.43

- Keeps sent messages above the composer, including multiline messages, rapid
  replies, and chat-window resizing. Automatic following waits for native layout
  and corrects lazy row-height estimates.
- Opens chat at the first unread message. Reading older messages keeps your
  position; a small new-message/Latest button returns to the conversation.
  Unread badges clear when the latest messages are actually in view.
- Keeps the message field focused after sending and preserves unsent drafts
  when no live room session is available.
- Removes the Talk/Ready text from the lower bar. The waveform, voice status,
  tooltips, accessibility labels, and existing voice controls remain available.

Includes native chat-viewport regression tests and preserves the durable room
sync and ALO rebrand from 0.13.42. Playback and voice transport are unchanged.
