## ALO 0.14.1

This patch makes the room experience more dependable and folds the latest room activities, chat, and voice controls into the public Mac build.

- Fix the album-colour playback seam when macOS reports a timestamp but leaves its elapsed-time value at zero. Progress now starts at the real track position, advances while playing, freezes while paused, and stays bounded to the track duration.
- Add the room Games library, including five distinct Rift Arena fighters, configurable bots, spectating, rematches, and verified downloadable presentation packs.
- Keep room navigation available in the compact controls and make Talk targeting explicit without adding persistent visual noise.
- Improve chat with typed mention suggestions, Command-K search, a smaller multiline composer, and clearer collapsed-message previews.
- Move microphone testing into Settings, add optional lyrics and Touch Bar room controls, and expose local audio-drift correction without changing another member's volume.

The separate nearby/iOS and shared-annotation integration remains in development and is not included in this Mac release.
