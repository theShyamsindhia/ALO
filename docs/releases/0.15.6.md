# ALO 0.15.6

Includes the screen-sharing improvements staged in 0.15.5:

- Keep shared video moving at longer negotiated playback delays, preserving the next frame due to display and the 64 MiB decoded-image budget.
- Avoid annotation redraws and presenter-window updates when only capture timestamps change.
- Start annotations with a compact Annotate control; open the tools when needed without removing existing shared annotations when they close.

This release also repairs the two verification blockers:

- Loopback test peers now use the same TCP endpoint-reuse settings as the actual app. A deterministic occupied-source regression verifies admission, while the existing non-reusable collision rejection and cancellation checks remain intact.
- The Chromium download fixture captures its first populated snapshot and closes its observation before teardown. Exact byte-count and 25% progress assertions remain; repeated and post-teardown callbacks have explicit coverage.

The socket-profile mismatch was reproduced with native Network.framework:
the old profile reported EADDRINUSE, while the production profile connected to
the same occupied-source setup. This does not claim to diagnose every possible
network-port conflict. Production networking, audio timing and buffering are
unchanged by the verification fixes.

Update both presenter and viewers. A physical two-Mac video session has not been
revalidated for this patch. Full Mac regressions, strict live timing, room
scenarios, signing, notarization and clean-machine distribution checks gate the
downloads. Version 0.15.6 supersedes the held 0.15.5 prerelease without replacing
its existing tagged build.

Validation: [verification](https://github.com/theShyamsindhia/ALO/actions/runs/34379536430)
and [release](https://github.com/theShyamsindhia/ALO/actions/runs/34379535849)
both passed for this candidate without reruns: 385 XCTest cases, 1,393 Swift
tests, and seven room scenarios. Downloaded archives also passed local signature,
Gatekeeper and checksum checks; their hashes match the published assets.
