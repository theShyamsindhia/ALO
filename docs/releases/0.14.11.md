# ALO 0.14.11

Regenerates every alternate app icon against a chroma-green canvas and removes the canvas with edge-connected transparency, eliminating the visible square background and lower border in Finder, notifications, and Settings.

Includes the latest Notch settings, room artwork and lyrics, game-window, connectivity, capture-clock recovery, and automatic alternate-icon preload changes from main.

Hardens local room networking against loopback endpoint collisions and preserves bounded audio service history until a sender has fully drained, preventing recovered links from over-admitting queued audio.

Repairs automatic updates from older ALO builds with a link-free updater ZIP, and gates future releases against archive entries those updaters cannot install.
